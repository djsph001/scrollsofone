1. LOGLINE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A conscience who works the locks refuses the President's stage, builds an
offline verification practice that can survive without him, and risks aid to
Haiti to keep help from becoming capture.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. DRAMATIC QUESTION AND CENTRAL CONFLICT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Dramatic question:
Can conscience constrain power without becoming power — can you refuse the
stage and still protect people?

Central conflict:
ONE (fifties, undiscovered artist, trained political thinker) practices
constraint as method: receipts, deadlines, audits, documented exit ramps.
"I can't command the river; I can work the locks." President CARMICHAEL —
Sandi's father — practices constraint as theater: if you control what
counts as reality, you control what people will endure.

The inciting collision is intimacy: Sandi, inside the regime, is assigned
to map One as a threat and instead recognizes a man who cannot perform;
she becomes pregnant and refuses to let that child be used as prophecy by
him or dynastic asset by her father. From that refusal outward, every
system is tested.

Spine:
Each sequence asks the same question at rising cost and answers with a
smaller, more irreversible action: refusal -> love -> mandate ->
counterfeit -> offline method -> personal weather -> narrative war ->
signature. The climax is not a speech or hearing. It is whether One signs
a single line that would trade verification for access.

Scripture and THE VOICE: sparing and dramatic. THE VOICE heard three
times only, same sentence, no source identified, no caption, no
explanation. OLDER ONE (V.O.) is the retrospective human narrator.
Generic VOICE (O.S.) is not used. Scripture only as short cited
epigraphs where canon places them.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. PRINCIPAL CHARACTER ARCS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ONE — The Conscience Who Refuses the Stage
  Want: To not lie, to not become what he hates, to protect people without
  turning them into symbols. Method is procedural friction, boring paperwork
  that outlives charisma. Tired, funny, bitter, still tethered.
  Arc: Clean refusal is enough -> regime breaks rooms not arguments ->
  builds infrastructure that does not need him -> pays price to keep it
  clean. Climax action: executes a pre-authorized circle refusal of one
  captured line, at cost, on their turf. Ends older, on his back, on top
  of covers — having made it harder to lie, not having won.

SANDI CARMICHAEL — The Bridge Who Chooses a Side
  Want: To soften damage from inside. Governing fiction: "If I'm in the
  room I can temper it; if I leave someone worse takes my chair."
  Arc: Withholds key insight from file on One -> refuses prophecy/
  succession use of pregnancy -> collapses bridge at interrupted meeting
  (father dismisses Haitian mother as "illegal"; blood only matters when
  connected to your name) -> draws privacy line (41) and chooses silence
  over clapback (49). Climax action (indispensable, grounded in BIO_SANDI
  / SCROLL_SANDI_02 / SCROLL_SANDI_04 and her training in communications,
  data, behavioral science): obtains the portal-enrollment annex missing
  from One's packet via a contact who will pass it only to her, withholds
  it from intermediaries, transmits it to One + Helen/Delphine with
  enforceable directive: "If this annex moves with the term sheet, child
  images and ID-linked harvest move with aid. Strip it or we walk." This
  annex converts stance into procedural standing.

THE BAPTIST — The Voice You Can Test
  Want: To keep One human. "I don't need you to be a prophet. I need you
  to be honest."
  Arc: Gives Black Box Rule and copy warning -> is taken -> procedural
  absence is horror. Trial of Two Voices dramatized as Sandi in lamplit
  kitchen detecting which clip breathes. Re-enters only once, late, as
  analog sentence on paper (Scene 29) via detainee chain. Function:
  verifies person and steadies method — does not decode policy. Trap is
  detected by Sandi's policy literacy + One's term-sheet literacy.

PRESIDENT CARMICHAEL — The Man Who Turns Survival Into Anointment
  Want: To govern story not country. "We curate which stories count as
  the country."
  Arc: Watches crisis on mute (sound=leverage) -> bullet miss decided as
  miracle/destiny -> rewrites losing as theft -> when spectacle fails,
  authorizes Project VOX ("continuity of guidance") synthetic Baptist.
  Antichrist function is procedural: authoring conscience's voice. In
  climax he is absent man, present trap — his Resilience Corridor terms
  (portal/trickle/two wings + compliance patch) are his character on paper.

Bureaucratic approval.

Exhaustion strategy.

THE VOICE — 2/3 — after empty chair. One alone, same sentence from no
direction. Tests as Baptist taught: does not obey, does not worship.

SEQUENCE 6 — THE COST AND THE COUNTERFEIT  [73:00 - 88:00]
Spine: What does power do when culture moves offline? It makes weather
personal and offers care that captures.

Scare: threat cancels meeting. No headline. Voice note -> "Not tonight."
Small, deniable, effective.

Response protocol plain text from librarian account: "If a meeting is
disrupted, we reroute. We document. We continue." Discipline: don't let
power train your nervous system.

Cost compressed into one volunteer household — not Sandi's — inspections,
job pressure, housing squeeze, weaponized welfare check. Clipboard, form
wanting too much. Help requiring identity, loyalty, or silence.

Regime imitates: Community Stability Program — care-with-capture. Stolen
compassion, counterfeit aid. Someone takes bait; data capture ->
punishment. Comfort as tactic (warm lights/cottage echo) + capture as care
(portal that helps and harvests).

One writes the rule at Auntie Jo's table, Sandi across, hand on stomach,
soup simmering, radio low. Titles aloud, testing: FOURTH BRANCH ETHIC:
Care without capture. Truth without spectacle. Method over charisma.
Redundancy, put it in writing, protect children, don't shame exhaustion.
Manual not scripture. Sandi adds line in her hand about child — no names,
no images in any circle material. Helen writes without debate, then feeds
people.

During this drafting, One also writes the single-page Fourth Branch
working draft for deliberation. Its language tracks SCROLL_GOVERNANCE_01
but is offered as draft, not law.
Circles coordinate without centralizing: Council of Kitchens — mycelium,
quiet signals, shared templates, distributed redundancy. "Moment you
centralize, you create a neck that can be cut."

SEQUENCE 7 — THE HEARING AND THE JOKE  [88:00 - 101:00]
Spine: What does power do when culture persists? It tries narrative war
and contempt war.

Trial balloon: friendly outlet floats "analog cells" — single paragraph,

First public hearing staged like democracy: seal, microphones, clock, live

OFFLINE EXTREMIST CELLS. They want One in archive — edit, isolate,

One refuses again: one paragraph, plain background, text-only, comments
disabled — same language as Seq 1 with cost behind it. Council sends no
leader. Sends Standard Kit — checklist, ethic, minutes — read by no one
in particular. No face to break; hearing breaks itself trying.

Comedy weapon: clean-suit comedian — "Apparently people are meeting in
secret... to read checklists." Graphic: WITNESS CIRCLES(tm). Crowd roars.
Verification becomes "cringe." Attendance dips from social punishment.
Ridicule as repression.

Sandi chooses silence: offered clapback slot, refuses. Locks baby privacy
harder, doubles on infrastructure. Quiet logistics scene with One: who
sleeps where, who is seen with whom, what pregnancy can carry publicly.
Love vs mission as logistics. Joke backfires late not by counter-joke but
by persistence — circles keep feeding people after monologue ends.

SEQUENCE 8 — THE TERMS OF SILENCE  [101:00 - 118:00]  |  Convergent climax
Function: All four active forces converge on one hand-marked line.
Stage 3 concretely visible (draft in collective custody), not enacted.
Chronology: CANONICAL 64->65->66->67; CINEMATIC 64->65->67->66.

The Haiti strand becomes throughline. One receives White House/UN envelope:
Haiti Resilience Corridor — National-International Resilience Initiative,
requested presence: One. No mention of circles. Just Haiti. Respect as
trap. Sandi names it. Team maps it: go -> "he ran"; stay -> "he abandoned
mothers who crossed the water twice." No painless option.

DRAFTING THE TERMS OF SILENCE (64) — domestic. Three intermediaries who
never admit they know each other: Brooklyn Haitian pastor (buried too many
boys), diaspora shipper (invoices and rumors), Signal voice: "They will
meet you. No cameras. No entourage. No speech. One hour. Neutral ground."
Team drafts boring terms: no recording, no aid-with-capture, no child
names or images, documented audits and exit ramps. Sandi holds the
portal-enrollment annex she alone obtained and does not share it with
intermediaries; it travels only to One and the kitchen custodians.

During this drafting, One prepares the Fourth Branch working draft and
executes the transfer: Before departure, One places one page — working
draft + ethic + checklist — in plain envelope, hands jointly to Helen and
Delphine as Council of Kitchens custodians: "This does not belong to me.
Copy it. Correct it. Decide whether it deserves a next version. Do that
without me." Three kitchens copy it that night. First markup happens while
One is absent. No law passes; custodians named; he cannot control edits.

THE BAPTIST'S SIGN arrives domestic side before departure — single analog
sentence on paper via detainee chain. Sandi verifies handwriting. Function:
proves verification method still works, steadies room, marks cost. It does
not decode policy.

THE DEPARTURE (65). Passport, itinerary, cheap pen. "If you leave they'll
say you ran." One decides not to prove anything. Small bag, no motorcade.
Sandi before he goes: "Do not go to prove anything." The walk line is
already authorized by the table.

HAITI — THE TERMS OF SILENCE (67). Driver outside. No bag. No phone. One
hour. Empties pockets, places phone on table. Spare room, on their turf.
No speech. Term sheet marked by hand: portal and trickle, two wings,
compliance patch. Counterpart presents conditioned version — access/trickle
conditioned on portal-enrollment annex that would route aid through
identity-linked harvest and retroactive content review of circles. Trap
is legible because Sandi's annex names it line-by-line; without that
paper, the condition reads as routine compliance.

One has standing because Sandi's annex + circle ethic establish what the
line does. He strikes the annex-linked line, initials margin, refuses to
sign conditioned version, offers clean version back. It is not accepted,
not rejected on camera, left on table. Lock worked, not triumph.

Cost with dignity: Delayed tranche holds. No immediate trickle cancels it.
Prior trickle acknowledged as already distributed, not as offset. Named
Haitian distribution steward — present since Scene 28 — is told "next
cycle, conditioned or clean" and chooses with One to wait for clean: "We
have waited longer for worse reasons. We will do this without selling the
children." Cost is shared decision, not passive collateral. Film holds the
cost through return.

Return/restore: One returns. No press conference. He walks the old
neighborhood (66) AFTER Haiti — cinematic 64->65->67->66 order. Hoodie,
paper bag, no cameras, visibility as violence. Passes mural, doesn't stop.
To show up. Fourth Branch ethic in body: care without capture. Meanwhile,
the working draft's first markup by other hands is shown — one line
amended without One — demonstrating collective custody and his lack of
control.

Final image: One alone in higher-ceilinged room from Prologue, fifties,
on back, on top of covers. Invitation gone. Checklist on nightstand.
Sonogram face-down, private. OLDER ONE (V.O.) — not THE VOICE — closes:

  "We did not win. We made it harder to lie, steal, and disappear people.
   That was the assignment."

THE VOICE — 3/3 — under that line, barely audible, without comfort,
without claim: "You are not alone in this conversation." One does not
answer. Camera does not decide what sentence is. Hold ceiling. Cut to
black. Title cards as Prologue: "Faith is not about certainty. It is
about trust." THE VOICE OF ONE.

Runtime: ~118 minutes.
