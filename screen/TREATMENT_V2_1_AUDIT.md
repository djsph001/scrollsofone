1. FINAL SOURCE CORRECTION — PROLOGUE LANGUAGE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Controlling Prologue language as now confirmed:

  "Before they called him dangerous.
   Before they called him prophet."

OLDER ONE (V.O.) narrates retrospectively about his younger self; changing
the cue does not authorize changing "him" to "me."

V2 and V2.1 presented the Prologue as:

  "Before they called me dangerous.
   Before they called me prophet."

and treated "me" as verbatim fountain delivery. That reading was incorrect.
The controlling wording retains "him" everywhere — both in third-person
summary paraphrase and in quoted OLDER ONE (V.O.) delivery. V2.1's Defect 2
ruling (distinguishing "me" as verbatim vs "him" as paraphrase) is
therefore superseded. No [ADAPTATION-ONLY] flag applies to the "him"
to "me" change because that change is not made — "him" is retained.

  - Invention register I23 / traceability notes updated to reflect "him" as controlling, not paraphrase.

Defect 2 — Prologue claim:

  RETIRED (from V2): "Before they called me dangerous. Before they called
  me prophet." — presented without qualifier.

  CORRECTED (V2.1 ruling, now superseded in detail — see Section 1 above):

  Prologue quotation is verbatim "Before they called me dangerous. Before
  they called me prophet." only when the line is diegetically delivered
  by OLDER ONE (V.O.) as written in
  screen/PROLOGUE_V2_THE_ONE_WHO_REFUSED-production.fountain (lines 15-16:
  "Before they called me dangerous." / "Before they called me prophet.").

  In the treatment's own prose summaries, where the narrator is described
  in third person, the form "Before they called him dangerous. Before they
  called him prophet." is not verbatim and is therefore not presented as
  quotation. Where the treatment dramatizes OLDER ONE (V.O.) speaking in
  first person ("Before they called me dangerous"), that delivery IS
  verbatim fountain text and requires no [ADAPTATION-ONLY] flag because it
  is the fountain's own first-person speaker (OLDER ONE is One, older).

  V2.1 applied this distinction consistently:
    - Quoted V.O. delivery: "Before they called me dangerous." [verbatim fountain]
    - Summary paraphrase: Before they called him dangerous [not quoted, not flagged as verbatim]
  No altered-then-quoted-as-verbatim text remained under that ruling.

  FINAL RECORD: V2.1's "me" verbatim claim is not retained. Controlling
  language is "him" in all positions. V3 corrects this. The V2.1 text
  above is preserved here as history, not as current authority.
