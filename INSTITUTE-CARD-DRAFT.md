## Framing notes (from Dale, 2026-06-13)

1. **Eyebrow first** — "Fiction · The Narrative Wing" signals genre before the title.
   Reader knows what they're looking at before reading the lede. This line protects
   both properties. Don't let it get dropped — its redundancy is the point.

2. **Lede ties fiction to through-line** — "how a person stays sovereign when
   systems are built to capture them" is verbatim Institute framing (Alloy Framework
   etc.). Makes the fiction read as the same inquiry in another key, not a side hobby.

3. **Canon first, novel second** — Canon (free, explorable, own domain) is the
   front door. Novel (KDP) is the destination for hooked readers. Leading with
   the canon signals exploration, not a transaction.

4. **Firewall note** — "It is not a statement of Institute policy or doctrine"
   lets serious readers enjoy the Antichrist novel without wondering whether the
   think tank literally believes it's living in the end times. Cheap insurance.

5. **Style** — Not a card under Books. Its own distinct section with different
   visual treatment from research/framework cards, so no one mistakes the novel
   for a white paper.
