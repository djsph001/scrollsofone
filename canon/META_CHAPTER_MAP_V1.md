Chapter Map (tight) — where each scroll/scene lands in the arc

ARC 1 — The Spark: Reality is Editable
	•	Scroll of the Baptist II–IV (copies, disappearance, “Trial of Two Voices”)
→ establishes the synthetic-voice threat + the need for verification.
	•	Scrolls of Carmichael I–IV (Mask, Bullet, Stolen Election, Synthetic Gospel)
→ shows why the regime’s default tool is spectacle + control.
	•	Scrolls of Sandi I–III (life inside performance → the file on One → private pregnancy moment)
→ positions Sandi as the bridge who understands the machine from the inside.

ARC 2 — The Public Flashpoint: Pregnancy becomes a battlefield
	•	Scroll of Sandi IV: When Social Media Discovered the Pregnancy
→ the world weaponizes intimacy; “quiet coalition” seed appears.
	•	SCENE_PUBLIC_FALLOUT_PREGNANCY_DISCOVERY (if separate from Sandi IV)
→ dramatizes the media war + meme economy + restraint culture.

ARC 3 — Refusal Doctrine: One rejects the stage
	•	Scroll of One: On Spectacle and Power
→ One’s defining refusal: no debate, no performance, no weaponized child.
	•	Scene: Carmichael Hears the Scroll
→ the regime reacts; control becomes personal.
	•	Scene: Sandi Between Two Voices
→ Sandi’s inner split (father vs. One) crystallizes.
	•	SCENE_ONE_CALLS_HIS_MOTHER
→ re-anchors One’s humanity and moral center.

ARC 4 — Proof vs Flood: Verification is attacked
	•	(Your new sequence) Hash-verified release + edited versions compared
→ verification method introduced.
	•	SCENE_32_THE_FLOOD
→ the regime floods “raw” clips to exhaust verification and make truth feel impossible.

ARC 5 — The Offline Pivot: Witness Circles are born
	•	SCENE_33_THE_CHAIN (first circle: church kitchen)
→ “human checksum,” open boring method, no recording, no hero.
	•	SCENE_34_THE_SECOND_CIRCLE (library version)
→ method travels beyond one community; becomes replicable infrastructure.
	•	SCENE_35_CARMICHAEL_FEELS_IT
→ Carmichael realizes the threat moved offline: culture > content.

ARC 6 — Soft Repression: Make the weather personal
	•	SCENE_36_THE_SCARE (threat cancels meeting)
→ intimidation without headlines.
	•	SCENE_37_THE_RESPONSE_PROTOCOL
→ reroute discipline; safety without spectacle; “don’t let power train your nervous system.”
	•	SCENE_38_THE_COST
→ inspections/job pressure/housing squeeze/welfare check weaponized.

ARC 7 — Fourth Branch becomes real: Mutual Aid + Ethic
	•	SCENE_39_THE_MUTUAL_AID_BRANCH
→ support network forms (legal/work/housing/care), no fame, no central pot.
	•	SCENE_40_THE_STOLEN_COMPASSION
→ regime imitates with “Community Stability Program” (care-with-capture).
	•	SCENE_42_THE_COUNTERFEIT_AID
→ someone takes the bait; data capture leads to punishment.
	•	SCENE_43_ONE_WRITES_THE_RULE
→ Fourth Branch Ethic codified: care without capture, truth without spectacle, method > charisma, redundancy, put it in writing, protect children, don’t shame exhaustion.

ARC 8 — Narrative War: Extremism frame + hearings
	•	SCENE_44_THE_TRIAL_BALLOON
→ “analog cells” smear test; circles respond with boring openness.
	•	SCENE_45_THE_COUNCIL_OF_KITCHENS
→ circles coordinate without centralizing; standards travel like recipes.
	•	SCENE_46_THE_FIRST_PUBLIC_HEARING
→ televised spectacle attempts to force One into the archive.
	•	SCENE_47_ONE_REFUSES_THE_STAGE_AGAIN
→ One declines; the Council speaks anonymously through the Standard Kit.

ARC 9 — Contempt Weapon: Comedy as repression
	•	SCENE_48_THE_COMEDY_WEAPON
→ ridicule makes verification “cringe,” attendance drops via social punishment.
	•	SCENE_49_SANDI_CHOSES_SILENCE
→ Sandi refuses clapback; locks baby privacy; doubles down on protection + infrastructure.
