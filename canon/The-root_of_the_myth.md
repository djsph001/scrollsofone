Canon Blurb: “Read the Book!”

(cleaned, sharper, publishable — still you)

READ THE BOOK.

This book is built on a dangerous idea:

This is the reign of the Antichrist.

It is a work of fiction—but it circles real questions: power, faith, technology, fear, love, and the hunger for certainty when the world feels unhinged.

At the center is a conflict:

Jesus is on the way.
But not so fast.

Because another figure is already here—
a prophet on Earth, preparing the return of Jesus.

And the question is simple, and terrifying:

Which one is the son of Jesus… and which one is the Antichrist?

⸻
The root of the myth 

Narrator

As a philosopher—not a political scientist, not a preacher—
I’ve made it my responsibility to ask one thing:

Is One truly the son of Jesus?

If he is, I don’t envy him.

Remember what happened the last time someone was called “Savior”?

And if he is not—
if he is the Antichrist, or the one I dare not name—
then the battle for Earth has already begun.

⸻

The Proposition

One’s offer is a peaceful transition.

If world powers agree to relinquish their grip,
One claims he can guide mankind back toward Eden.

But first he must prove to humanity
that he is who he says he is.

And for that—

you’ll have to read the book.

⸻

This story is delivered as an epic poem—sometimes classical, sometimes whimsical, sometimes raw and strange. It refuses to stay in one genre, because the world it describes refuses to stay in one mood.

Regards,
One
