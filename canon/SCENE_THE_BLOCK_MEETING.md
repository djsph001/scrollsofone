SCENE: “THE BLOCK MEETING”

He offers something tangible: a small fund for school supplies or legal fees or a repair day — but he insists it’s run by locals, not him.
People still suspect him… until a respected elder says:

“He didn’t ask to be crowned. He asked where to carry the boxes.”

The rule for his arc

He earns trust by losing control.
The community doesn’t become his “base.” It becomes his accountability court.
