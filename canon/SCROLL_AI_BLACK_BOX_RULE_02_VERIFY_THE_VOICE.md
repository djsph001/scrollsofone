Scroll of AI & Power: Verify the Voice
(A Public Protocol Posted by One)

If you are hearing a voice you trust—
and something in you feels off—
do not panic. Do not share. Do not feed the machine.

Verify.

We are entering an age where:
	•	voices can be copied,
	•	faces can be worn,
	•	and “statements” can be manufactured faster than truth can travel.

So here is a simple protocol—human-scale, repeatable, and clean.

THE VERIFY-THE-VOICE PROTOCOL

1) Assume nothing.

Not even my voice.
Not even his voice.
Trust is earned by proof, not familiarity.

2) Look for the convenience.

Ask one question:

Who benefits if I believe this right now?

A fake is usually designed to create relief, rage, or permission.

Truth is rarely that convenient.

3) Require the signature.

From this moment forward, any real message from me will include:

a private phrase known only to my inner circle,
placed in an ordinary sentence—
not as a password, but as a fingerprint.

If you do not see the signature, treat it as unverified.

4) Demand the source chain.

If someone posts “exclusive audio,” ask:
	•	Where did it come from?
	•	Who recorded it?
	•	When?
	•	What is the unedited original?

If the chain is missing, the clip is not truth—it is theater.

5) Refuse the viral test.

Do not “spread it so others can judge.”

That is how the machine wins.

Send it quietly to people you trust.
Compare. Confirm. Then speak.

6) Use the old rule.

If it cannot survive daylight, it is not meant to guide you.

THE POINT

This is not paranoia.

This is literacy.

A civilization that cannot verify its voices
will eventually be ruled by the loudest counterfeit.

So hold the line:

Don’t chase the feed. Build the test.
Don’t worship familiarity. Require proof.
Don’t weaponize uncertainty. Practice restraint.

And remember:

The still small voice is not subject to majority rule.
