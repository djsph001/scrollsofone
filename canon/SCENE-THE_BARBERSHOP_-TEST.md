SCENE: “THE BARBERSHOP TEST”

He walks in and nobody greets him warmly. They talk around him.
A guy says the quiet dagger line:

“You don’t sound like you’re from here. You sound like you’re passing through.”

One doesn’t defend. He says:

“You’re right. So tell me what ‘from here’ requires.”

Then he shuts up and listens for 20 minutes.
