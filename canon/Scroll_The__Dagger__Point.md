Scroll: The Dagger Point

I did not fear the punishment meant for me.
I feared the punishment that would find the ones I love.

There is a special kind of sorrow
in realizing your conscience has a shadow—
and it falls on someone else.

So I confess it plainly:

I hate what my truth has cost her.
I hate the way power reaches for love
as if love were evidence.

And still—
I will not lie to keep the peace of the cage.

If I must carry a dagger,
let it be this one:
that love is not safe,
but it is real.

And the real is worth defending.

⸻
